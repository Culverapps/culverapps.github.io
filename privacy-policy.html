VibeCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-13

VibeCheck is a cross-cultural dating and relationship coaching app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language; if anything is unclear, contact us at culverapps@proton.me.

1. Who we are

VibeCheck ("we", "us") operates the VibeCheck mobile app for iOS and Android.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and a display name you provide at sign-up.
  • Profile data: the answers you give during onboarding (age band, the region/culture you're dating in, optional coaching goals and starting point). These are used only to calibrate the coaching tone and are private to you.
  • Conversation data: the messages, bios, transcripts, or profiles you submit for coaching. These are processed to generate your coaching response and are tied to your account.
  • Usage data: message counts, Spark balance, and feature usage, used to enforce plan limits and improve the product.
  • Crash & analytics data: anonymous diagnostics (screen views, errors). We do not use these to identify you personally.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide coaching responses and personalise tone to your situation.
  • To enforce plan limits (daily message caps, Spark balances).
  • To secure your account and prevent abuse.
  • To understand aggregate product usage and fix problems.

4. Spark credits

Sparks are an in-app credit. A small number are granted daily; additional Sparks are purchased. Sparks are non-refundable and expire only if your account is closed. Purchases are processed by the App Store / Google Play; we do not store your payment card.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Sub-processors needed to run the service (our database host Supabase, our AI model provider for generating coaching, and the app stores for billing).
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Your conversations are sent to our AI model provider solely to generate your coaching reply. We do not train public models on your personal conversations.

6. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time from the in-app Account screen. On deletion we remove your profile, conversations, and usage records. Backups are purged on a rolling schedule (typically within 30 days).

7. Children

VibeCheck is for users 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 17.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. Exercise these from the app or by emailing culverapps@proton.me.

9. Contact

culverapps@proton.me

10. Changes

We'll post material changes here and, where required, notify you in-app.
