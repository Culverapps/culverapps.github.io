Privacy Policy

VibeCheck — operated by Culver. Effective date: 16 August 2026.

TL;DR: We collect the minimum needed to coach you — your account, the messages you send us, and any photos you choose to upload for review. Payments are handled by our billing provider (RevenueCat / the app stores). You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app.

1. Who we are

VibeCheck is a communication and confidence coaching application operated by Culver. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the data controller. You can reach us at the contact details in Section 7.

2. What we collect

a) Account data

  • Email address and a user ID (created when you sign up).
  • Your selected coaching preferences and region settings.

b) Conversation data

  • The text messages you send to the coaching chat, and the coach's responses.
  • These are stored so your conversations persist between sessions and so the service can provide continuity.

c) Photos you upload

  • If you use Photo Review or upload a profile photo, the image is uploaded to our secure storage (Supabase) and analysed by our AI coaching model to give feedback.
  • Photos are stored in a private bucket; they are not publicly accessible and are not used for advertising.

d) Purchase data

  • Subscription and consumable (Spark) purchase events are processed by RevenueCat and the relevant app store. We store your entitlement status so we can unlock features. We do not store your card number.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and personalise the coaching service.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To analyse photos you upload, with your explicit action, for the Photo Review feature.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • To respond to support requests.

4. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. If you cannot use the in-app control, email us (Section 7) and we will action the deletion within 30 days.
